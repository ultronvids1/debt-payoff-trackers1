How it works

Two popular payoff strategies — pick one that matches your personality.

❄️

Snowball

Pay off the smallest balance first to build momentum. When a debt is gone, roll its minimum payment into the next.

🏔️

Avalanche

Pay off the highest interest rate first to minimize total interest. Usually the cheapest mathematically.

🔒

Private

No account required. Your plan is saved locally in your browser. Clear it anytime with reset.

FAQ

Is this really free?

Yes — the core tracker is free. Pro will add exports (PDF/CSV), multi‑plans, and nicer visuals.

Do you store my data?

No. Your entries are saved in your browser (localStorage). If you switch devices or clear browser data, it resets.

Is the math exact?

It’s an estimate based on monthly compounding and your minimum + extra payments. Real statements can differ slightly due to daily interest and fees.
